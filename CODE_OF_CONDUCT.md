# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a place for careful, technical work. Everyone taking part —
issue reporters, contributors and maintainers — is expected to treat the others
with respect, whatever their background or level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and be kind. Critique the code, the claim or the design, never the person.
- Assume competence and good faith; ask before concluding.
- Accept that a claim without evidence will be challenged, and that this is the
  norm here rather than an insult.
- Respect a decision to close a thread; a maintainer's time is finite.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal or political attacks,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **abhinavsharma11@hotmail.com**. Reports are read in
confidence. A maintainer may warn, remove a comment, or block an account, and
will say which and why.
