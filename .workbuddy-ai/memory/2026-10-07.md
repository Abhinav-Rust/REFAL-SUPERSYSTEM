**Mechanism the tree reversal needs (for the next session).** The bracket-field
`Rev` cannot reduce: `<Rev (Node <Rev (e.R)> <Rev (e.L)>)>` is stuck because the
pattern's bracket field meets a *call*. Unfolding it needs: abstract a
bracket-valued stuck call to a fresh bracket `(c)`, unfold, and carry the
definitional link `<Rev (e.R)> = (c)` so that `<Rev (c)>` renders back as
`<Rev <Rev (e.R)>>`. Then the pair becomes
`<Rev<Rev (e.L)>> <Rev<Rev (e.R)>> = (e.L) (e.R)` and each component folds to the
claim — soundly, because the fields are then in the domain.

accepts; `open` is honest, and the abstraction would re-enable the exact fold
